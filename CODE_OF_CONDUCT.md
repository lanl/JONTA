# Code of Conduct

JONTA contributors are expected to maintain a welcoming, technically rigorous,
and respectful community.

Expected behavior includes:

- communicating constructively and assuming good faith;
- focusing criticism on equations, code, evidence, and decisions rather than
  people;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting errors openly;
- respecting different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation,
personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information
without consent.

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Reports
will be handled confidentially where practical and reviewed promptly. Project
maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or take other action
needed to protect the community.
