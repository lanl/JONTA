# Dreicer generation

Small-angle collisions and electric acceleration are active. Synchrotron
radiation is a controlled on/off parameter; large-angle collisions and plasma
coupling are disabled. The benchmark reports runaway production rates versus
electric field and effective charge.

References are Guo et al. (2017) and the analytical runaway-rate literature
cited there, including Kulsrud et al. (1973), Dreicer (1959), and Connor &
Hastie (1975). Exact paper panels and acceptance intervals will be recorded
before the first production run.
