# Avalanche and decay

Small-angle collisions, electric acceleration, and large-angle collisions are
active. Synchrotron radiation is an explicit scan parameter. The benchmark
measures exponential growth or decay, threshold field, cutoff dependence, and
marker/thinning convergence.

Primary references are McDevitt, Guo & Tang, *PPCF* 61, 054008 (2019), and
McDevitt, Guo & Tang, *PPCF* 60, 024004 (2018). RAMc Sec. III.E / Fig. 15 is a
legacy cross-check.

Digitized Appendix B3/B4 and Fig. 13 values are in `reference/`. The driver and
YAML configuration are pending migration from the legacy benchmark scripts.
