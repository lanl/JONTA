# Radial transport

Circular 1-D guiding-center transport benchmark based on McDevitt, Guo & Tang,
*PPCF* 61, 024004 (2019). Compare radial convective transport and diffusivity
for fully ionized and partially screened cases, including the published
uncertainty bars and analytical scaling relations.

Digitized data for Figures 3 and 6 are in `reference/`. The driver and YAML
configuration are pending migration from the legacy validation driver.
